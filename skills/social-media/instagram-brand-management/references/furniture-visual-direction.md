# Furniture Photography Visual Direction

Derived from production reference images (dark/warm minimalist aesthetic).

## Aesthetic: Modern Minimalis + Warm Tones

### DO
- Dark/warm neutral background (charcoal #2C2C2C, concrete, abu tua)
- Warm wood tones front and center (natural oak #D4B896, walnut #8B6F4E)
- Soft warm ambient lighting (lampu kuning, golden hour)
- Tanaman hijau as accent color
- Karpet/tekstil for warmth
- Context shot — furniture IN a styled room
- Portrait 4:5 ratio (1080x1350) for IG feed
- Consistent warm-toned editing (lifted shadows, slightly desaturated)

### DON'T
- White studio background (catalog vibe, kills lifestyle feel)
- Flash photography (flattens wood texture, looks cheap)
- Bright overexposed (destroys warm mood)
- Too many props (cluttered, distracts from product)
- Watermark besar
- Bird-eye/overhead angle (uncomfortable for furniture)
- Isolated product on flat background

## Feed Grid Pattern
```
[Lifestyle] [Product]  [Edukasi]
[Product]  [Lifestyle] [Testimoni]
[Edukasi]  [Product]  [Behind Scene]
[Lifestyle] [Testimoni] [Product]
```
Rule: Max 2 product shots in a row. Alternate with lifestyle/education.

## Reel Style
- Duration: 15-30 sec
- Music: Lo-fi, ambient, ASMR wood sounds
- Transitions: Match cut, whip pan, fade to black
- Text: Hook text in sec 1-3 only
- Speed: 1.5x for assembly, 1x for room tour
- CTA: Last 2 sec — "DM untuk order"
