## Company-Specific Micro Analysis Framework

For each stock, evaluate:

1. **Valuation**: P/E vs historical average and peers
   - P/E < 10: CHEAP
   - P/E 10-15: FAIR
   - P/E 15-25: PREMIUM
   - P/E > 25: EXPENSIVE

2. **Growth**: Revenue and earnings trajectory
   - Revenue growth > 15%: STRONG
   - Revenue growth 5-15%: MODERATE
   - Revenue growth 0-5%: SLOW
   - Revenue growth < 0%: DECLINING ⚠️

3. **Profitability**: ROE and margins
   - ROE > 20%: EXCELLENT
   - ROE 15-20%: GOOD
   - ROE 10-15%: ADEQUATE
   - ROE < 10%: LOW

4. **Balance Sheet**: Debt/Equity ratio
   - D/E = 0: ZERO DEBT (excellent)
   - D/E < 30: CONSERVATIVE
   - D/E 30-60: MODERATE
   - D/E 60-100: ELEVATED
   - D/E > 100: HIGH LEVERAGE ⚠️

5. **Dividend**: Yield assessment
   - DY > 8%: VERY HIGH (check sustainability)
   - DY 5-8%: ATTRACTIVE
   - DY 2-5%: MODERATE
   - DY < 2%: LOW (growth-focused)

6. **52W Position**: Where is price in its yearly range
   - < 20%: NEAR BOTTOM (value opportunity)
   - 20-40%: LOWER HALF
   - 40-60%: MID-RANGE
   - 60-80%: UPPER HALF
   - > 80%: NEAR TOP (overbought risk)

7. **Volume**: Current vs average
   - > 2x avg: SPIKE (institutional activity)
   - > 1.5x avg: ABOVE NORMAL
   - < 0.5x avg: LOW (lack of interest)
