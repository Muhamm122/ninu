# Working Example: @haus_living1 (Haus Living — Furniture/Mebel)

## Account Profile
- **Username:** @haus_living1
- **Brand Name:** Haus Living
- **Previous name:** @nana.furniart (rebranded 2026-06-05)
- **Niche:** Dark Modern Furniture / Home Living
- **Style:** Modern Minimalis + Warm Wood Tones (dark backgrounds, warm ambient lighting)
- **Target:** Urban Indonesia, 25-45, menengah atas
- **Voice:** Warm, honest, aspirational, premium tapi approachable
- **Brand concept:** "Haus" (German: house) + "Living" — premium cross-language name, European feel matches dark/modern aesthetic

## Rebrand History
- **2026-06-05:** Rebranded from @nana.furniart → @haus_living1
- **Reason:** "furniart" was generic, didn't match dark/modern aesthetic. "Haus Living" signals premium, international, modern.
- **Username choice:** `haus_living1` chosen because `hausliving` and `haus.living` were unavailable or unchecked
- **Directory:** `~/.hermes/haus-living/`

## Brand Visual Assets (2026-06-05)

### Logo
- **Selected version:** V4 Wordmark — bold "HAUS" + italic "Living" (cleanest, most scalable)
- **Variations generated:** 5 (house icon, monogram HL, sofa icon, wordmark, geometric H)
- **Colors:** Dark bg (#1A1A1C), Honey accent (#D4A574), Terra accent (#C4735B)
- **Typography:** Georgia/serif for premium feel
- **Format:** SVG source → PNG 1080×1080 via rsvg-convert
- **Location:** `~/.hermes/haus-living/logo/`

### Watermark Tool
- **Script:** `~/.hermes/haus-living/watermark.py`
- **3 types:** catalog (15% corner stamp), content (65% bottom bar), reel (70% top-left badge)
- **Tech:** Pillow/PIL — no GPU or API required
- **Batch mode:** `python3 watermark.py batch <folder> --type catalog`
- **Brand config:** Edit BRAND_SHORT, BRAND_NAME, COLOR_HONEY at top of script
