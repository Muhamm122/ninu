## Photography Style

### DO ✅
- Natural light only (golden hour kalau bisa)
- Shot dari sudut yang nampilin ruang penuh (context > product alone)
- Styling dengan tanaman, buku, kopi — lived-in feel
- Clean background (white wall / concrete / light wood floor)
- Consistent editing: warm tones, lifted shadows, slightly desaturated
- Product detail: close-up tekstur kayu, stitching, joint

### DON'T ❌
- Flash photography (flat dan murahan)
- Barang di atas karpet merah / backdrop putih studio (ke-katalogan)
- Watermark besar (kecil transparan OK)
- Over-edit / HDR (kayu harus nampak natural)
- Foto dari atas (bird eye) — ga nyaman buat furniture

## Feed Aesthetic (Grid Pattern)

```
Row 1: [Lifestyle] [Product]  [Edukasi]
Row 2: [Product]  [Lifestyle] [Testimoni]
Row 3: [Edukasi]  [Product]  [Behind Scene]
Row 4: [Lifestyle] [Testimoni] [Product]
```

**Rule:** Max 2 product shot berturutan. Sisip lifestyle/edukasi biar ga jadi katalog.

## Reel Style Guide

|---------|-------|
| Durasi | 15-30 detik (optimal) |
| Music | Lo-fi, ambient, ASMR wood sounds |
| Transition | Match cut, whip pan, fade to black |
| Text overlay | Minimal — hook text di detik 1-3 saja |
| Speed | 1.5x buat assembly, 1x buat room tour |
