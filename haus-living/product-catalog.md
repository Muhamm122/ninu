## 🎨 Visual Direction (Berdasarkan Refensi Lo)

### Aesthetic: **Modern Minimalis + Warm Tones**

Berdasarkan gambar referensi yang lo kirim:
- **Background gelap/tile tua** — kontras dengan furniture
- **Warm wood tone** — dominan natural oak / walnut
- **Clean lines** — ga ada ornamen, straight edge
- **Lifestyle styling** — tanaman, kopi, buku, karpet, bantal
- **Low-key lighting** — warm ambient, bukan bright studio

### Foto Style Reference

```
DO:
✅ Dark background (abu tua / charcoal / concrete)
✅ Warm wood tones front and center
✅ Soft warm lighting (lampu kuning / golden hour)
✅ Tanaman hijau sebagai accent
✅ Karpet / tekstil buat warmth
✅ Context shot — furniture di ruangan, BUKAN isolated

DON'T:
❌ White studio background (ke-katalogan)
❌ Flash photography (flat)
❌ Bright overexposed (kacauin warm vibe)
❌ Too many props (cluttered)
❌ Watermark besar
```
