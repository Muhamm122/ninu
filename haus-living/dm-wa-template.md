## WA Funnel Flow

```
IG POST/REEL (awareness)
    │
    ▼
IG STORIES (interest — swipe up / link)
    │
    ▼
DM CONVERSATION (consideration — tanya harga, size, dll)
    │
    ▼
WHATSAPP (conversion — katalog, negosiasi, order)
    │
    ▼
PAYMENT (Midtrans / Transfer / COD)
    │
    ▼
CONFIRMATION + ETIMATED DELIVERY
    │
    ▼
DELIVERY UPDATE (foto barang sebelum kirim)
    │
    ▼
POST-DELIVERY (foto di rumah customer → testimoni → UGC)
    │
    ▼
RETENTION (promo bulanan, koleksi baru, referral)
```
